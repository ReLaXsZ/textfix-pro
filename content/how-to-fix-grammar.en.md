Writing error-free text is crucial, whether you are writing an email to your boss, a school essay, or a social media post.

Here are a few practical tips to improve your grammar:

## 1. Read out loud
When you read your text out loud, your ear will immediately catch the words that sound strange or are in the wrong tense.

## 2. Use AI tools
The fastest way to check your text is to use an AI tool. 

In [TextFix Pro](/) there is a built-in **"AI Grammar"** button. Just paste your text, click the button, and the AI will not only fix the mistakes but also explain what was wrong.

## 3. Break up long sentences
Long sentences confuse the reader. Try to split any sentence that has more than 20-25 words.
